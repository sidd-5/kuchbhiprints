    The 3D difference

    Every object starts as an idea and grows one layer at a time.

    Hundreds of thin layers stack up to make each piece. That’s why our objects can have lattices, spirals and hollow shapes that would be hard to mould. Look closely and you’ll see the lines that show how it was made.

    1. 01

      Layer by layer

      Each piece is printed from the base up. The fine ridges on the surface are part of its character.

    2. 02

      Shapes moulds can’t make

      Jaali lattices, twisting ribs and hollow forms are where 3D printing does its best work.
